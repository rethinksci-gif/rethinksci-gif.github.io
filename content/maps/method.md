> **The question this map is organised around:**
> What evidence would change the next engineering decision?

## A reading path

1. **Define the decision.** Start with [[engineering-case-notes/index|Engineering Case Notes]] and identify the outcome that matters.
2. **Separate observations from hypotheses.** Follow [[engineering-case-notes/abs-to-pcr-pp-electroplating-diagnosis|the ABS → PCR PP case]] to see how a surface defect becomes a set of testable questions.
3. **Choose the next experiment.** Read [[material-intelligence/case-study/ai-materials-rd-closed-loop|the materials R&D loop learning note]] for the relationship between prediction, evidence, and decisions.
4. **Return to the source.** Use the linked references in each note or publication to inspect assumptions and limits.

## How to read this garden

- **Seed:** an early question or reference; gaps remain.
- **Growing:** a developing argument with connected notes and open questions.
- **Evergreen:** a more stable explanation, still open to correction.

These labels describe note maturity, not an independent verification of every claim. Engineering cases and literature learning notes should be read with their stated scope and limitations.

## Questions to investigate

- Which uncertainty has the largest effect on the decision?
- What is the smallest comparison that could disprove the current explanation?
- Are two alternatives being compared on the same functional and system boundaries?
